The change management problem nobody talks about.

AI adoption isn't like previous technology changes. You're not replacing a workflow — you're replacing what feels like the person. That triggers a much stronger resistance than a new CRM or a new dashboard ever did.

If you try to change the workflow before you solidify the person in their role, the rollout fails. My approach addresses both sides: the human layer and the technical layer.
